# Feedback interpreter gold annotation review

The frozen `1.0-draft` fixture remains the scoring authority for the v2 benchmark. The observations below are proposed annotation decisions for the gold-set owner. Each uses the written feedback and the original scope and routing rules as evidence. | FI-007 | “not interested in another core-banking replacement transformation” names a recurring project type. The fixture records a mild negative career-category preference scoped to core-banking replacement. | Retain the narrow preference for this benchmark. Confirm whether recurring project types belong to `career_category` or `role_responsibilities` in the product taxonomy. ## Related reference questions

- **FI-006** has the same evidence boundary as FI-035. “Even if I need training” qualifies explicit interest in AI enablement. Review these two required training signals together so they have a consistent standard.
- **FI-013** expects `PENDING_APPROVAL` with a reported eligibility violation. FI-023 and FI-028 expect `PROFILE_FACT_REVIEW` with the same hard-action code. FI-013 also retains a positive signal from “This could be fun,” which leaves the attractive responsibility unspecified. Confirm the precedence for a known eligibility violation alongside a clear independent preference, and decide what amount of detail makes a role preference actionable. Keep action, route, and preference annotations under separate review.
- **FI-049** retains the explicit consulting career preference while sending the unspecified objection to clarification. Keep this reference as a counterexample for any policy that drops independently stated preferences on a clarification route.

## Approval and versioning

Obtain explicit human approval for each reference change before editing any gold data. Record the accepted change alongside the case ID, prior and proposed annotations, quoted evidence, rationale, and approver. Publish approved changes in a distinct fixture version with its own fingerprint. Keep the original 50 cases and scores available, and label any results on revised references with their fixture version so benchmark comparisons remain reproducible.
