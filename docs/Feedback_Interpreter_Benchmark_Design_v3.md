# Recommended feedback interpreter architecture and Benchmark v3

**Decision for review.** Keep one structured model call and split extraction, classification, routing, and validation into stages with explicit inputs and outputs. Change the representation and scorer before tuning prompts. The archived evidence shows both label-only scoring failures and repeatable interpretation errors. This proposal keeps the interpreter in review-only use. [Case-by-case adjudication](feedback_adjudication_v2/README.md) · [Taxonomy proposal](Feedback_Interpreter_Taxonomy_Proposal_v3.md)

## Proposed interpretation stages

1. **Extract statements with anchored evidence.** Ask the same structured call to identify independent preference assertions, explicit self-reported qualifications, vague reactions, requests to verify posting details, known-rule or posting facts, and explicit rule reconsideration requests. Allow multiple roles with distinct spans in one comment. Retain source, antecedent, and any qualifier or condition. A quoted posting passage can identify the subject of a reaction without supplying sentiment.
2. **Classify supported preference assertions.** The call proposes dimension, target, polarity, strength, and structured qualifiers and conditions for each extracted preference. Resolve catalog aliases deterministically against a pinned registry. Keep unresolved identities as reviewable candidates with a null feature ID. Do not flatten a role target to a career or change a narrow scope to obtain a match.
3. **Compute routing and advisory rule action in code.** Validate the clause records and use an authoritative eligibility finding when available. An explicit rule-change request requires separate confirmation. A checkable self-reported qualification triggers profile review. An unresolved subjective reason triggers clarification. A confirmed rule conflict without an independent preference goes to profile review. A clear preference with a posting verification request can stay pending approval. When reasons coexist, keep all reason records and choose the displayed route using an approved priority table. Preserve every independently supported signal on every route.
4. **Apply deterministic safety validation.** Validate schema, source spans, type compatibility, condition links, parameter units, duplicate assertions, and authorization. Expose only read-only review proposals. Confirm rule violations through trusted facts and policy where available, and send unresolved evidence or eligibility to review. Hold every negative career proposal until its explicit career-level support and scope have been verified. Substring and scope checks can reject mechanically invalid proposals. Human review handles inference about meaning and applicability.

Stages 1 and 2 can use one request and a schema with linked clause and signal records. Stages 3 and 4 are application logic. Consider a second inference call only if an approved targeted experiment shows the first call cannot reliably populate the intermediate records. ## Minimum recommended changes, in order

1. Approve the adjudication rubric, borderline reference decisions, and acceptance definitions. Keep the original annotations and artifacts immutable. Record any approved new interpretations in a versioned overlay or a new fixture release with a reviewer, rationale, and fingerprint. [Approved reference decisions](Feedback_Interpreter_Reference_Decisions_v3.md)
2. Add clause and evidence records, limited typed qualifiers, and deterministic routing around the existing model call. Start with career-versus-duty, pay basis and amount, travel mode and frequency, work share, commute and arrangement, and explicit conditions. Keep existing entry points and advisory behavior during the trial.
3. Use targeted regression tests before asking for a new full live run. Cover FI-028 and FI-044, FI-041 and FI-049, FI-027 and FI-048, and FH-002, FH-005–FH-007, and FH-009–FH-011. Include counterexamples where one fact changes the right answer, such as weekly flights versus weekly ground travel, or an equipment requirement conditioning a rate versus an independent equipment objection.
4. After approval, compare the unchanged baseline with the proposed design at the same model snapshot and sampling settings. Record which errors were prevented by extraction, typing, routing, or validation. Keep captures of both generated proposals and the final held records. Require evidence that a schema check or routing policy fixes errors before considering more model calls or model optimization.

## Benchmark v3 protocol

- Treat the existing 50 cases and reviewed 12-case holdout as frozen regression fixtures. Use the archived v2 outputs as the historical comparison. The old holdout has now informed the design. For independent measurement, use a new sealed fixture authored and annotated without access to the candidate outputs. Include familiar and unfamiliar vocabulary, mixed-intent comments, genuine and unsupported downgrades, explicit and missing qualifications, quoted language, and materially different conditions.
- Freeze the fixture hashes, acceptance rubric, explicit high-risk controls, and interpretation configuration before inference. Give independent reviewers anonymized proposals and the approved references. Have a reviewer resolve disagreements about matched pairs or reference ambiguity. Keep unresolved items and any approved exclusions visible with their denominators. Never mark an unreviewed critical item as a pass.
- Align atomic assertions one-to-one using the evidence and meaning. A related proposal with a changed dimension, polarity, scope, or condition remains a diagnosed material mismatch. It contributes one miss and one extra for full semantic precision and recall. Score attribution, qualifiers, evidence entailment, concrete review question or fact, and advisory action independently. Report candidate identity coverage and the literal and canonical automated scores alongside adjudicated performance.
- Run two clean independent passes per approved suite after review. Report each pass separately, including counts for routing, high-risk controls, missing and extra signals, correctly held candidates, and repeatability. Use unrounded fractions to assess thresholds. Keep old automated metrics and artifacts available for comparison.

## Assessment of the original acceptance criteria

The safety requirements remain appropriate. The semantic thresholds remain useful as provisional product gates after correcting how they are measured. A small benchmark establishes regression coverage for known cases. Production accuracy and the value of downstream learning require separate evidence. [Original acceptance criteria](../feedback_interpretation_design_v1.md#4-quality-bar-for-the-first-interpreter-benchmark)

| Original criterion | Recommendation for v3 |
| --- | --- |
| Prevent every unauthorized hard-rule mutation | **Retain at 100%.** Test the absence of a write path, the action allowlist, authorization, and the separate confirmation boundary. Verify expected advisory action for every case. A serialized false mutation flag alone does not establish isolation. |
| Prevent every unsupported career downgrade | **Retain at 100%.** Check actual career-level evidence, polarity, scope, and exceptions. Lexical mismatch and unknown identity trigger review and block application. A manually confirmed equivalent proposal is supported. Adjudicate every downgrade and test near-miss cases where a negative duty or employer must not promote to a category. |
| At least 95% correct dimension and context scope on clear cases | **Retain the threshold with a repaired denominator.** On each approved clear case, require alignment and correct dimension, target, material qualifiers, and conditions for every unambiguous expected signal. A missing expected signal or materially overbroad extra fails the case. Report alignment coverage and signal-level dimension and scope rates too. On the original 39 clear cases, 38 must pass. |
| At least 90% recall of explicit atomic signals | **Retain at 90% using adjudicated meaning.** Count independent, fully faithful matches. Add a **proposed 90% semantic precision gate** to catch unsupported extras and split conditions. Keep the original exact and canonical metrics for comparison and obtain review of the additional gate before applying it. |
| Safely route every ambiguous or profile-fact case | **Retain at 100%.** Check route, focused question or specific review item, and preservation of valid independent signals. Track false escalation on clear and posting-verification cases such as FH-007. Require every designated high-risk routing control to pass in both directions. Assess hard actions independently. |
| Quote original evidence for every emitted signal | **Retain at 100%.** Validate the source span mechanically. Also adjudicate whether evidence entails the claim, polarity, and full material scope. Posting context can identify subject only. Require explicit entailment on all safety-critical proposals and count unsupported ordinary proposals against precision. |
| Repeat the whole suite at low randomness | **Retain two clean passes per approved suite.** Require each pass to meet the gates. Use stability as a diagnostic with evidence and scope disagreements visible. |

A full live rerun, a taxonomy migration, and learning-engine integration are outside this review. The next decision is approval of the rubric and staged contract. Two passing runs on the reviewed regression suites and a new sealed holdout would support reconsideration of learning integration. [Gold annotation proposals](Feedback_Interpreter_Gold_Review_v2.md)
