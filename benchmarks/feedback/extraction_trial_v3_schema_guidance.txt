For each signal, choose a scope type allowed for its dimension. A posting-level
reaction uses the posting scope only when the user independently evaluates the
posting as a whole. A clause that summarizes a narrower supported reaction
belongs with that reaction. Keep independently evaluated subjects separate.

When the evidence states an intensity that materially limits the preference,
use an intensity qualifier in machine scope and retain its exact evidence.
Use the following values for that qualifier.
- none for the complete absence of the scoped attribute
- near_zero for almost none
- low, moderate, or high for an explicitly supported level of the attribute
- strong for strong emphasis on a duty or attribute
- dominant when the evidence makes the duty central or predominant
Treat the values as qualitative. Preserve the amount of the attribute and
the strength of the user's reaction separately. Use the original words for
the qualifier's evidence quote. Leave the intensity qualifier out when the
feedback gives no supported level.
